A pan-tilt head for [107-systems/l3xz-hw-pan-tilt-head](https://github.com/107-systems/l3xz-hw-pan-tilt-head), designed for [Alexander Aentinger](https://github.com/aentinger).

The head sits on top of the L3X-Z platform and gives it eyes — two servos, a stack of 3D-printed structural parts, and the cable runs that have to survive the full sweep of motion without fouling.
